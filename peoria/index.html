Keeping Your Peoria HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
